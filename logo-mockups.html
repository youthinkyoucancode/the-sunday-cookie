Cookie + sun in a single icon. The visual pun is too good not to use — Sunday is right there, and so is the cookie. Best at small sizes. Doubles as your Instagram avatar and your packaging stamp. Most distinctive of the three.

A wax seal / postage stamp. Lives on every kraft box, every IG post corner, every email signature. Most ritualistic. Pairs beautifully with the "Sunday Letter" framing — every box arrives like real mail. Works best when packaging photography is the hero.
