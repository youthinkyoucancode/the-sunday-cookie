Our story

Twelve years of Sundays.

I was fifteen when I started baking, in Tunisia. Sunday was my one day off school, so I'd bake for my family — almost always cookies. They still remember the first ones.

The Sunday tradition came with me to Cologne in 2022. Twelve years of perfecting one recipe — brown butter, deep brown sugar, real chocolate in proper chunks — and I'm finally sharing it.

— Abir
